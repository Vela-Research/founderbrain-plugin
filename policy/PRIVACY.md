# FounderBrain privacy policy

The privacy policy for FounderBrain is published at https://thinkreasonlearn.com/privacy. That page is the only version, so it is never out of step with this repository.

In short, FounderBrain is provided by Vela Partners Management LLC. The transcript Claude sends is held in memory only while a read runs and is not stored, and the report goes back to Claude. Operational logs and hourly read counts are kept for a limited time, as the policy sets out. Questions go to support@thinkreasonlearn.com.
